# Behavioral Profile

## Overview
Niloy Sannyal's behavioral profile aligns with a **Builder / Pragmatic Architect** pattern. He combines strong algorithmic computer science fundamentals with an end-to-end builder mentality, demonstrating rapid self-onboarding onto new tech stacks and sole architecture ownership of enterprise systems.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Autonomy & Ownership | High | Thrives when given full architectural ownership of problems, from requirement analysis to production deployment and monitoring. |
| Learning Velocity | High | Rapidly absorbs and productionizes unfamiliar technologies (e.g. Odoo CRM, Qdrant, Next.js 15, Redis AOF) to solve practical problems. |
| Technical Versatility | High | Comfortably navigates across ML/LLM engineering, backend APIs, data pipelines, DevOps/VPS administration, and frontend dashboards. |
| Pragmatic Execution | High | Focuses on shipping reliable, low-latency production software that directly serves business operations and real users over theoretical perfection. |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **End-to-End Delivery:** Owns systems from 0 to 1—scoping, designing data models, building pipelines, Dockerizing, setting up CI/CD, and managing server reliability.
- **Client & Cross-Functional Empathy:** Bridges the gap between technical complexity and business reality, leading consulting engagements (e.g. factory digitization) and building intuitive admin tooling for non-technical stakeholders.
- **Algorithmic Rigor:** Solved 530+ competitive programming problems (LeetCode/Codeforces), ensuring system design decisions are grounded in solid time/space complexity and optimization practices.

## How You Work Best
- Fast-moving engineering environments with direct accountability and high autonomy
- Working on modern AI/LLM architectures, agentic pipelines, and full-stack software integration
- Collaborative teams that value shipping, pragmatic system design, and measured business impact

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Deep Delegation over Solo Execution:** Having historically succeeded as a sole architect and builder, continuing to expand delegation and cross-team mentorship patterns in larger engineering organizations.
- **High-Impact Orientation:** Prefers fast-moving product iteration and architecture design over bureaucratic maintenance of legacy codebases without clear impact.
